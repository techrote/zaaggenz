The actual v1.2.1 source archive is not present in the current mounted files or repository tree. ZG-001/G0 therefore remains open. All experiments will be isolated under `research/preflight-2026-09-12/`. They are testable research prototypes, not integrations into the missing instrument. Third-party audio stays outside Git; only identities, derived measurements and non-audio annotations may be published. No human responses or biochemical measurements will be invented.
