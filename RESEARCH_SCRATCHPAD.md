## Authority and boundaries

The owner requested as much useful preliminary research/precomputation as available tools permit. The live issue graph and uploaded `zaaggenz-programme-v1.zip` are inputs, not evidence of implemented software. At the time of this preliminary pass, the actual v1.2.1 source archive was not present in the mounted files or repository tree, so ZG-001/G0 correctly remained open **then**. It was subsequently recovered and authenticated; that later state is intentionally not rewritten into the historical experiment notes below. No baseline reconstruction, default changes, gate waiver or task closure is authorised by this research pass.

All experiments will be isolated under `research/preflight-2026-09-12/`. They are testable research prototypes, not integrations into the missing instrument. Third-party audio stays outside Git; only identities, derived measurements and non-audio annotations may be published. No human responses or biochemical measurements will be invented.

## Work queue

- Audit live tasks/overview and the uploaded bundle; preserve stable-ID mappings.
- Reproduce and extend local reference descriptors, with stereo/level/time support and ambiguous tempo candidates explicit.
- Review primary spectral modelling, dissonance, tuning, DSP and experimental-design sources.
- Compute independent fixtures for multiresolution/phase accuracy, band-delta identity, Chordness failure modes, tuning/roughness sensitivity, exact metrical clocks and research-design simulation.
- Produce implementation-ready notes, machine-readable results, tests, reproducibility manifests and issue-specific evidence pointers.

## Completion policy

Every result must say what was actually run, which inputs and method versions were used, what failed, and which claims remain untested. Integration and owner listening remain separate gates. The final report will link a reproducible runner and bounded next tasks rather than marking the whole programme complete.
