# Planning audit: risks, corrections and residual uncertainty

## Corrections already applied

**Do not optimise zaag into cleanliness.** The preferred reference edit was louder and, under the planning estimator, spectrally flatter than the original. Lower noise/roughness/clipping cannot be the global objective. Preserve useful aggressive source nonlinearity; distinguish it from accidental bus flattening.

**Do not claim spectral retuning as new.** Sethares-style spectral mappings/adaptive tuning and classical sinusoidal/residual methods are direct prior art. Zaaggenz's value is integration with persistent nonlinear zaag synthesis, source-preserving arrangement, phrase grammar and empirical tooling.

**Do not confuse metrical level with polyrhythm.** Fast/half/quarter-rate interpretations may be nested levels of the same pulse; true independent cycles are represented separately.

**Do not turn the research hypotheses into product blockers.** Phrase roles, linked fake-outs, gesture direction and vocal control can be useful creative features even if controlled studies yield null or listener-dependent effects.

**Do not pool incomparable research endpoints.** Expectation/surprise ratings, groove, movement, tension, pleasure, source identity and recognition are distinct. The programme uses separate studies/fields and only synthesises them conceptually where evidence permits.

**Do not let analysis silently alter the sound.** All analysis is observational until an explicit transform/apply action creates a new project revision.

## High-risk engineering areas

### Baseline provenance
The actual v1.2.1 source/archive was not available when the programme was written. ZG-001 is therefore a hard gate. Reconstructing production source from conversation snippets would invalidate regression claims.

### Phase/coherence in spectral transforms
Dense nonlinear zaag material may defeat clean component tracking. Low-confidence fallbacks, transient/remainder preservation and stereo/phase tests are mandatory. Never force every peak into a musical component.

### Target collapse / metric gaming
Chord-fit optimisers can cheat by muting energy, crowding target teeth, collapsing to one pitch or erasing roughness. Chordness therefore has movement/energy/coverage/residual constraints and must be auditioned.

### DSP graph complexity
A completely free graph early would multiply state/latency/migration problems. Start from typed bounded stages and explicit integration points; cycles require a separate future contract.

### Async stale-state faults
Long analysis/inverse jobs can return after the project changed. Every result needs a revision identity and atomic publication checks. Preview must keep priority under heavy analysis.

### Research overinterpretation
Prediction models describe the generator's uncertainty, not the listener's internal probability distribution. Subjective excitement is not a neurotransmitter measurement. Familiarity/culture/effort may moderate effects strongly.

### Reference-pair confounding
The Activation/Zaagtivation files differ in mastering, duration, arrangement and likely source processing. Use manual matched motifs and synthetic ablations; no unique producer-chain inference.

## Product risks

- Research UI could overwhelm the instrument: mitigate with Compose-first defaults and progressive disclosure.
- Full SYNTHLINE could disappear again in refactoring: protect with explicit stem/null tests.
- Global output normalisation could undo subtractive SCULPT or hide overload: retain one declared final gain/ceiling policy.
- Source-preserving rendering could become a hidden resynthesis mode: keep render mode explicit in recipes/tests.
- New defaults could drift from owner-approved sound: `locked_bloom` remains immutable and default changes need explicit review.
- Optional ML/GPU dependencies could become accidental requirements: deterministic CPU path is the baseline.

## Research risks

- Small-N/user-specific findings will not support universal claims.
- Repeated exposure can teach the stimulus while also reducing novelty; model session/order/familiarity.
- Vocal/movement manipulations add effort and self-generated sound, so any change in rating has multiple plausible causes.
- A dissonance minimum is not a cultural scale or preference optimum.
- Named traditional grammar packs can misrepresent living musical systems; default to synthetic descriptions until source review.

## Audit checklist before changing the programme

1. Does the change improve or at least preserve ordinary composition?
2. Does it alter a protected sound, stem, gain, phase, latency or project contract?
3. Is a proxy being mistaken for artistic quality or listener response?
4. Is there direct prior art that should change novelty wording or implementation choice?
5. Can the same evidence be shared through an existing immutable feature/render cache?
6. Does the dependency graph need a real new edge, or only a shared write lock?
7. Is the claimed completion backed by implemented/tested evidence rather than a plan, screenshot or issue state?
