# Empirical programme — concise implementation brief

Research capabilities must remain optional to ordinary composition. Creative usefulness and research validity are separate gates.

## Shared method

Use immutable stimulus recipes, content/method hashes, declared sample rate/tuning/stage order/output gain, frozen matching procedures, counterbalanced presentation where appropriate, predefined exclusions, explicit exploratory vs confirmatory status, and reproducible result artefacts. Audio descriptors measure audio; they do not directly measure a listener's biochemistry.

## Planned study families

1. **Reference-pair and synthetic zaag ablations** — align comparable motifs, then isolate spectral organisation, nonlinear placement, roughness distribution and melodic/phrase changes in controlled synthetic counterparts.
2. **Chordness / timbre-dependent tuning** — compare identity, reweight-only, retune-only and bounded hybrid transformations while measuring source identity, perceived sonority/fit and musical usefulness.
3. **Linked violation / recovery** — independently manipulate local expectation violation and recognisable return, including a linked case where the substitute event becomes the expected arrival.
4. **Variation windows / metrical anchors** — vary fill location, slower anchors and gesture directionality while keeping other material controlled.
5. **Vocal / embodied participation** — compare passive listening with imagined or audible self-chosen imitation while recording familiarity, fluency and effort as moderators.
6. **Unfamiliar grammar learning** — teach short synthetic tuning/modal grammars, then test recognition of departures, returns and phrase functions across exposure.

## Analysis boundaries

Predefine primary contrasts and uncertainty reporting before confirmatory collection. Use repeated-measures/mixed-effects methods when listeners or stimuli recur. Null or inconclusive findings are valid completion outcomes and must not be silently redefined into success.

Raw third-party audio remains private unless redistribution rights are established. Commit recipes, hashes, synthetic fixtures, documented methods and suitably aggregated research artefacts rather than source recordings.

Detailed protocols, statistical scaffolding and literature boundaries are indexed through `docs/zaaggenz/RAG_INDEX.md` and the source register.
