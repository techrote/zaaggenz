# ZG-010 sonority progression and constrained voice-leading

ZG-010 adds an explicit harmonic construction layer above ZG-007 tuning and ZG-008 source-preserving note rendering. It does **not** infer chord names from spectra and it does not collapse voice-leading, acoustic fit and modal/grammar preference into one objective.

## Sonority coordinates

A `SonoritySpec` has an explicit root degree and 1–12 named tones. Each tone is one of:

- a tuning-relative integer `degree_offset`, optionally with cents detune; or
- an explicit positive frequency `ratio` relative to the root, optionally with cents detune.

The two coordinate modes are mutually exclusive. Ratio tones retain their source coordinate while also exporting the nearest ZG-007 tuning degree + detune needed by the existing note-event contract. This permits JI-like or other ratio sonorities inside octave and non-octave tunings without pretending the ratio originated as a scale degree.

Tones may be required or optional. `bass_tone_id` requests an inversion by constraining the **lowest moving voice** to that tone. A fixed/pedal anchor does not silently become the inversion voice.

## Stable voices and layer roles

A `VoiceSpec` has a stable ID, a declared layer role (`sub`, `body`, `aux`, `synthline`), a hard frequency range, preferred register, maximum inter-frame leap and anchor policy.

Anchor policies are:

- `moving`: normal voice-leading;
- `hold-first`: the first frame is solved normally, then that exact frequency is retained on later frames;
- `fixed-hz`: explicit fixed frequency, useful for a low anchor/pedal that should remain independent of changing sonority roots.

A held/fixed voice does not count as satisfying a later frame’s required sonority tones. If the remaining moving voices cannot cover the required tones, solving fails. This is intentional: a persistent layer may coexist with harmony without being mislabelled as a current chord member.

## Deterministic solver

For every moving voice, candidate pitches are generated by tuning-period/register shifts and clipped to the voice’s frequency range. Candidate enumeration is explicitly bounded by `candidate_cap_per_voice` (128 by default). The solver then uses dynamic programming across low-to-high voices.

Hard constraints:

- voices are strictly ordered and cannot cross/equal;
- optional minimum spacing;
- hard voice frequency ranges;
- hard maximum inter-frame leap;
- every required sonority tone must appear in a moving assignment;
- inversion request applies to the lowest moving voice;
- fixed/held anchors are exact.

The optimized cost is only:

`movement_weight × sum(|voice movement cents|) + register_weight × sum(|distance from preferred register|)`.

The result stores movement cents, register cents and voice-leading cost separately. `acoustic_fit_cost` and `grammar_cost` remain `None`; ZG-014/ZG-018 and ZG-011 own those domains. A later Pareto or weighted search can combine them explicitly, but ZG-010 does not smuggle an acoustic proxy into “better harmony”.

## Target comb and downstream transforms

Every frame exposes two frequency sets:

1. `fundamental_targets_hz`: exact solved voice fundamentals;
2. `target_comb_hz`: bounded harmonics of those fundamentals, sorted and deduplicated within 0.5 cents.

The target comb is intended as an explicit downstream target for ZG-014 descriptors and later ZG-017/ZG-018 spectral transforms. It is not a chord classifier and not a quality score.

## Events and current layer-integration boundary

`progression_events()` converts solved voices to frozen PhrasePlan-compatible note events while preserving stable voice IDs, exact nearest tuning degree/detune coordinates and declared layer roles. This provides the contract surface later issues can route into persistent BODY/AUX/SUB/SYNTHLINE ownership.

ZG-008 currently renders synthline events only. `audition_progression()` therefore renders each stable voice as its **own source-derived SYNTHLINE recipe**, preserving voice identity in separate stems, then combines those diagnostic stems with a shared downward-only headroom attenuation if needed. It does not pretend that the legacy BODY/AUX/SUB engines already obey the new harmony state; ZG-029 owns that integration.

## Fixture families

The tests/report cover:

- ordinary 12-EDO triad shapes;
- explicit inversions;
- moving bass;
- hold-first and fixed low anchors;
- chromatic approach-and-return roots;
- optional colour tones/open voicings;
- non-octave synthetic 13-ED3 coordinates;
- explicit ratio tones;
- impossible spacing/coverage failures;
- stable role/event IDs;
- source-derived multivoice audition through the actual ZG-008 renderer.

These are mathematical/engineering fixtures, not declarations that one harmonic vocabulary is musically superior. Cultural/modal directionality belongs to ZG-011 and later grammar work.
